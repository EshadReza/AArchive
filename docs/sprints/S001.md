## External gate G01

The owner's [ZIP inventory](../archive-layout.md) is already supplied and preserved
verbatim. “Actual archive support” means parsing and validating the exporter’s
records according to trusted definitions, not asking for the folder tree again.

Missing inputs are the schema bodies, their provenance/version/draft, concrete
field/identifier/unit semantics and a permitted representative sample. The
[intake checklist](../archive-contract.md) defines what S025 needs. G01 gates S025
and its dependency descendants; it does not block S001 or independent foundation
and manual-grading work. No exporter field names or production schemas were
invented here. Deployment access and later integration contracts remain gates on
their own cards, as listed in ADR 0006.

## Contract review cases

These are documentation consistency checks, **not executed application tests**.
The implementation sprints above must prove the behavior with runtime tests.

- Two installations using the same local contest/user IDs remain separate by
  namespace; a submitted namespace claim alone grants no authority.
- Re-uploading an earlier accepted version creates no new source version and
  leaves the current pointer unchanged; superseding a version never rewrites its
  producing job's success status.
- Team participants require no arbitrary individual owner; a later team member
  gains neither earlier private source access nor another member's marks.
- One Contest can produce independent assessments in two Offerings. Teaching one
  Offering permits its grading without granting global contest mutation rights.
- PUBLIC or group membership cannot expose personal marks, private teacher notes,
  or restricted evidence. Field permissions cannot bypass hidden parent gates.
- A separately confirmed pending-account creation survives import cancellation;
  cancellation still leaves no published contest rows or confirmed mapping.
- Preparing or failing a correction leaves the published mark visible; locked
  marks cannot change until elevated audited unlock.
- Eligible source purge retains university marks, history and provenance through
  tombstones; it does not delete a blob still required by a retained reference.
