## Delivered

- Offering referencing a Course, Batch, Term and Section, with a unique tuple,
  matching-parent PostgreSQL trigger and immutable identity/creator/creation time.
  Active context is required within the shared privilege-locked write transaction.
- Eligible Teacher/Admin creation attaches its creator through audited membership
  creation. The actual Offering policy scopes Teachers to current assignments;
  counts, details and writes use that policy. All current co-teachers share name
  settings rights. Admin-only creators gain no implicit Teacher platform role.
- Admin-only staff assignment/removal and archive, reviewed in the UI. Effective
  assignment history survives removal, with immutable provenance and ended rows;
  reassignments append a new row. Removal immediately revokes teacher scope and
  existing offering grants without changing offering authorship. Admin global
  authority survives removal of their own assignment.
- Paginated offering/history/staff APIs, strict request fields, same-origin CSRF,
  active academic selection routes and revision conflicts. Historical assignments
  expose display names/UUIDs/times, with email limited to the admin staff picker.
- `/offerings` list/create and `/offerings/:id` settings/history pages, linked from
  Account for Teachers/Admins. Named parent selection resets dependent choices.
  Archive and staff changes require review; errors clear stale controls and require
  reload. Mobile staff selectors are constrained to their panel width.

See [offerings](../offerings.md) for exact APIs, authorization matrix, transaction
boundaries, models/triggers, archive semantics and local walkthrough. README,
architecture, API/database/access/audit/security, academic/catalog and D03/D04 notes
are updated. No enrollment, grading, contest authority or archive import is added.
